Karo Recon

Fee reconciliation that actually understands how parents pay.

M-Pesa, Equity, KCB, Co-op — every payment matched to the right student automatically, with siblings, typos, and messy references handled correctly. Anything uncertain waits for a human, never a guess.

◆

Handles siblings correctly

When one phone pays for two kids, it asks — it never silently guesses which child the money belongs to.

⎈

4 banks, one system

M-Pesa, Equity, and KCB confirm payments in real time. Co-op is checked automatically every few minutes.

⚠

Nothing touches a balance blindly

Only confident matches auto-apply. Everything else sits in a review queue for a real person to confirm.

🔒

Encrypted, per-school

Every school's bank credentials are encrypted separately — one school's keys are never visible to another.

Built for Kenyan schools · term-aware, CBC-ready, multi-bank from day one
