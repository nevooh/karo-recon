Direct-to-bank fee reconciliation for Kenyan schools

Every fee payment, matched to the right student, straight from your bank.

When a parent pays into your school’s account, your bank tells Karo Recon. We read the admission number, find the student and update their balance. No statements to download. No Excel. No guessing.

How a payment is handled

1

Parents pay as they always have

Through your bank’s paybill or M-Pesa. Nothing changes for parents, and nobody has to learn a new way to pay.

2

Your bank tells us directly

The notification comes from your bank to your school’s own private address. There is no middleman and no statement to upload.

3

The balance updates

The payment is matched to the student and their term balance drops. If a payment could be two children, or none, it waits in a short review list for a person to decide.

Works with your bank

We connect directly to your school’s bank and to M-Pesa. Using a bank we haven’t connected yet? Tell us when you apply. We work with your bank to set it up, and we confirm with you whether it can be done before anything starts.

What your school gets

◆

Siblings handled correctly

When one phone pays for two children, the system asks. It never silently decides which child the money belongs to.

⚠

A review queue, not guesses

Uncertain and unmatched payments wait in one place with the reason they were held, until a real person decides.

☰

Fees, terms and balances

Fee structure per class, terms, optional fees and balances brought forward, so each student’s balance is worked out from your own fee set-up.

▦

Reports and an activity log

See what was collected, what is outstanding, and who changed what and when. Every important action is recorded.

🔒

Private to each school

Each school’s payment keys are encrypted separately and never shown again after they are saved. Staff have their own sign-ins and roles, and sensitive actions ask for a PIN.

📱

Parent portal Coming soon

Parents will be able to check their children’s balances, payments and receipts from their phones.
